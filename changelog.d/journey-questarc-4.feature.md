Journeys offer everything QuestArc 4 does: stay at a station a while or pass one you already do, a gentle way back after a long break, letting a habit graduate and bringing it back, pairing a step with something you enjoy, an optional plan check, tying a new step to a habit, and QuestArc's texts in German.

Starting a journey lets you pick any start date and change what it builds, and a habit whose task no longer repeats can get a task again from the shelf.
